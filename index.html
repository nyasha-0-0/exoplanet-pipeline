01 — Data EngineeringPHASES 1–8

150,000 stars. Zero data leakage.

NASA's Kepler Space Telescope monitored over 150,000 stellar targets, generating millions of cadences. The primary detection mechanism is the transit method: periodic dips in flux proportional to area ratio: $\Delta F / F \approx (R_p / R_\star)^2$.

Historically, candidate Threshold Crossing Events required exhaustive manual inspection—consuming ~80% of human vetting bandwidth. EXOSCOPE implements an automated, fault-tolerant ingestion engine that pulls quarters Q1–Q17 from the Mikulski Archive for Space Telescopes (MAST), phase-folds each candidate, and bins it into dual views.

Equipped with atomic progress persistence (processed_kepids.json) and exponential backoff retry wrappers, the pipeline ensures resilient ingestion across thousands of targets.

SAFEGUARD 01

Transit-Preserving Masking

Calculates transit boundaries ($t_0 \pm 1.2 \times \text{duration}$) and masks out transit points during spline/median detrending, preventing ingress distortion.

SAFEGUARD 02

Linear Interpolation

Avoids unphysical step discontinuities caused by zero-filling missing cadences. Uses linear interpolation across masked intervals with zero-median normalization.

SAFEGUARD 03

Star-Level Splitting (Zero Leakage)

Enforces grouped splitting on Kepler ID (KEPID). Multi-planet systems never span train/test sets, stopping the CNN from memorizing host star noise.

SAFEGUARD 04

Asymmetric Outlier Clipping

Aggressive suppression of stellar flares ($+3\sigma$) while preserving deep physical transit dips down to $-15\sigma$, guarding true planetary signals.

SAFEGUARD 05

Platt Scaling Calibration

Maps raw CNN uncalibrated logits into true Bayesian posteriors via held-out logistic regression, reducing the empirical Brier score from 0.1921 to 0.1814.

05 — Production RoadmapPHASES 12–15

From research pipeline to survey deployment.

Four successive engineering phases transitioning EXOSCOPE into an automated mission-scale triage platform for NASA TESS and ESA PLATO.

PHASE 12

Secondary Physics Vetting

Automated pixel-level centroid motion checks (identifying background eclipsing binaries via center-of-light shifts) and stellar parameter consistency audits.

PHASE 13

Injection-Recovery Testing

Injecting synthetic transits into quiet Kepler cadences to empirically map pipeline detection completeness across planetary radius $R_p$ and period $P$.

PHASE 14

Cross-Mission TESS Generalization

Evaluating the Kepler-trained Dual-View CNN directly on NASA TESS 2-minute cadence light curves without fine-tuning to measure cross-instrument domain shift.

✓ PHASE 15 (ACTIVE)

Web Tool Deployment

In-browser ONNX inference with WebAssembly and interactive physical validation sheets, deployable directly on Vercel with zero server overhead.
